Why Franchise with But First, Coffee?

Making good coffee accessible to more Filipinos.

We have helped aspiring entrepreneurs turn passion for coffee into a business by giving our franchise partners the opportunity to operate under an established homegrown coffee brand  with a proven concept, effective system, ongoing support and expert guidance across store construction, branch operations, staff training, marketing and promotions, supply chain, human resource management and business compliance. 

Who We are

Powered by Francorp

Backed by Francorp, a global leader in franchise development and consulting, But First, Coffee is built on proven franchising principles, professional systems, and a strong foundation for sustainable growth. This partnership reflects our commitment to empowering entrepreneurs with a structured, scalable, and well-supported business model.

Award-winning.

In 2024, But First, Coffee was recognized as the Most Promising Filipino Franchise at the Franchise Excellence Awards and was inducted into the Francorp 100 Club—milestones that reinforce our vision of building a world-class Filipino coffee franchise.

At the 2026 7th VP Choice Awards, the Coffee Chain of the Year was awarded to But First, Coffee, amidst strong competition.

Franchise Formats

Choose a format that fits your vision, location, and investment goals. From full-service In-Line Stores to flexible Indoor/Outdoor Trailers and Indoor/Outdoor Kiosks, But First, Coffee offers scalable franchise options designed to bring your coffee business closer to your market.

In-Line Store

A full café experience designed for high-visibility locations, offering a spacious setup for customers to dine, work, and enjoy their favorite But First, Coffee drinks.

Indoor/Outdoor Kiosk

A compact and efficient format designed for smaller spaces while delivering the same quality coffee experience with a streamlined setup.

Indoor/Outdoor Trailer

A flexible and mobile format ideal for high-foot-traffic areas, events, and strategic locations—bringing But First, Coffee closer to your market.
