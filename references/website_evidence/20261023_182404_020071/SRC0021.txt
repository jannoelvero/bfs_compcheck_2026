A: Scouting for a suitable location is the applicant’s responsibility. However, our team will assist by assessing your proposed site to ensure it meets our standards for approval. Q: What is the minimum space requirement for a Potato Corner franchise? A: We require a space of at least 6 square meters with a minimum frontage of 2 meters . This allows sufficient working space for smooth operations. Q: How long does it take to achieve ROI (Return on Investment)? A: On average, franchise owners achieve a return on investment within 30 months. Q: What is the average cost of goods sold (COGS)? A: The average COGS is around 53% , resulting in a gross margin of approximately 47% . Please note that net income will still depend on operational expenses. Q: What are your available payment options? A: We require full payment upon contract signing. You can choose from: Cash or check Online payment or bank transfer (for one-time payment only) Q: How long does it take to open a franchise from application to approval? A: Once all requirements are completed, you can expect your store to open within 30 to 60 days . Q: What is the renewal fee? A: The renewal fee is 50% of the current franchise fee . With the current franchise fee set at P150,000 , the renewal fee is P75,000 , extending your contract for another five years. Q: Do I need to be a corporate employee to own a franchise? A: Absolutely not! You can apply as a sole proprietor, part of a partnership, or a corporation . Q: Does Potato Corner charge royalty fees? A: No, we do not charge any royalty fees !
