Franchise Details

TRADEMARK
Famous Belgian Waffles

OWNER & OPERATOR
INNOVENTION FOOD RESOURCES INC.

NATURE OF BUSINESS
To sell freshly baked Belgian Waffle Sandwich and Hot & Cold beverages

STORE TYPES
Small fit in, Cart, Kiosk, Counter & Inline Type

TERMS OF FRANCHISE
3 years (subject for renewal)

IDEAL LOCATIONS
Malls, Commercial Areas, Cafeterias, Business Hubs, Transport Terminals, Schools, Airports, Hospitals, corporate offices
