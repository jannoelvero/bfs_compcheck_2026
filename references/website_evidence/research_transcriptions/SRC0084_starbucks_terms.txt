50 Stars: customization or selected bakery products.
100 Stars: handcrafted drink or bakery product.
150 Stars: pasta, sandwich, or cake slice.
Listed exclusions include ice cream based beverages, Breve and 2oz Heavy Cream at 50 Stars; Traveler Kits and French Press at 100 Stars.
Reward applies to the highest priced eligible item.
Gift certificates, Starbucks Cards and card reloads do not earn Stars.
On redemption purchases, Stars accrue on the amount paid using the registered card; the redeemed item does not earn Stars.
